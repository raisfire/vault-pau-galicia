1. Answer the two subsections in this question. (2.5 points) 
1.1. After reading the text, indicate whether the following statements are True (T), False (F) or the 
information is Not Given (NG). In true and false cases only, copy the part of the text which justifies 
your answer. (1.5 points) 
1. Two or three decades ago, mostly men—and, exceptionally, some good-looking women—were hired 
to work as disk jockeys. 
2. Thanks to the Internet, the DJ-ing scene has changed, to the extent that LGBTQ+ are being hired on 
a regular basis. 
3. Online platforms are owned by straight males, which indicates that society hasn’t really changed 
much. 
4. Women do not feature in the dance music genre because they are not interested in it. 
5. Not a single female girl DJ hit number 1 in the UK in the first to decades of the 21st century. 
6. The music industry clearly has double standards and doesn’t treat males and females as equals.
