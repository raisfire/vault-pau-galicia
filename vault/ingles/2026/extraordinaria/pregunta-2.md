2. Answer the two subsections in this question. (2.5 points) 
2.1. Write a NEW sentence that has the same meaning as the given one. Use the word or expression 
in brackets. (1.5 points) 
1. She is too tired to study. (slept) 
2. He is such a good musician that everyone admires him. (so) 
3. It’s a pity you didn’t tell me. (wish) 
4. “Don’t forget to call me”, he said. (reminded) 
5. He didn’t study. That’s why he failed. (if) 
6. I’m sure they didn’t hear the news. (can’t) 
2.2. Answer the following questions about pronunciation. (1 point) 
1. Which of the following pairs of words are pronounced the same? fear/fare, bare/bear, dear/dare, 
pear/pier, fill/feel. 
2. In which of the following words does the stress fall on the second syllable? empire, control, rumour, 
basically, famous. 
3. In which two words is the –ed morpheme pronounced as the underlined letter in “bread”? washed, 
folded, judged, refused, masked. 
4. In which word do we pronounce the letter “i” as in “filling” (i.e., short i)? align, bite, kind, bitter, family.
